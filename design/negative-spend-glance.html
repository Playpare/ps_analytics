Why 90 days and not two weeks. A D7 verdict needs cohorts that have reached day 7, and a D28 verdict cohorts that have reached day 28. The old two-week default contained none of the latter, which is why every payback figure on the page read zero. Ninety days is the shortest window that judges both — and it fits inside the 100 days the server already ships, so it costs no extra request.

Mockup. Network names, campaign names, the $0.80 D7 target, Google Ads being judged on budget alone and TikTok's unusable install data are all real. The 90-day figures are illustrative — judge the layout and the reading order, not the numbers.
Colour. Cost/revenue pair validated on both surfaces: all checks pass. Status fills pass band, chroma and contrast, with CVD separation in the floor band — which is why every status carries an icon and a word, and both series are named in the key rather than left to colour.
